About Our Project

Our project involved designing and launching a water bottle rocket using recyclable materials, water and compressed air.

The goal was to travel at least 6 metres while keeping the rocket below 50 cm and using non-hazardous materials.

Our final rocket measured approximately 42 cm and achieved 31.2 metres in its first competition trial.
